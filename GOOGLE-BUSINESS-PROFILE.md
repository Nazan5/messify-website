# Messify — Google Business Profile setup

Google Business Profile (GBP) decides who appears in the map results when someone near Academic City, Silicon Oasis or International City searches "meal plan near me" or "meal plans Dubai". It matters more for local ranking than anything on the website. Set it up once, then spend 15 minutes on it every week.

Everything below is ready to copy and paste.

---

## 1. Create or claim the profile

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account Messify will own long-term (not a personal one you might lose).
2. **Business name:** `Messify`
   Use the name exactly. Do **not** add keywords like "Messify Meal Plans Dubai". Google suspends profiles for keyword-stuffed names.
3. **Business type:** choose the option for a business that **delivers to customers**.
4. **Address:** enter the address where the Messify team actually works during business hours. Google doesn't allow P.O. boxes (so not the Ajman P.O. Box), virtual offices, or a partner's premises where Messify has no staff or signage. If your team packs and dispatches from the partner kitchen in International City every day, with Messify branding on site, that address can work. Confirm this before you start, because getting it wrong is the most common reason profiles are suspended.
   If customers don't come to the kitchen, **hide the address** and show service areas instead. That's Google's rule for delivery businesses, and breaking it risks suspension. Google still uses the hidden address to judge distance, which helps you in Silicon Oasis and Academic City because they're close.
5. **Verification:** Google usually asks for a short video. Film in one take: Messify branding at the address, your team at work (packing bags, dispatching), and proof the business is yours (your Messify FZC trade licence).

## 6. Services (this is where your plans and prices go)

You'll only see this option after the profile is verified.

**How to add them (computer):**
1. Sign in to the Google account that manages the profile and search Google for **my business**. Your profile opens with a row of buttons at the top.
2. Click **Edit services**. (If you don't see it, click **Edit profile** and look for **Services**.)
3. Click **Add more services**, then **Add custom service**, type the service name and save.
4. Click the service you just added to fill in the **price** and **description**.
5. Changes made from Google Search save automatically.

**On your phone:** open the Google Maps app, tap your profile picture, open **Your Business Profile**, then **Edit profile → Services**. Button names can shift slightly between app versions.

Google's rule: no prices, phone numbers or links in a service *name*. Put the price in the price field.

## 7. Products (optional: dishes, not plans)

Google's product rules don't allow **subscriptions**, so don't add the meal plans as products; they could be rejected. Use Products as a photo menu of signature dishes instead.

1. On your profile (search **my business**), click **Edit products**, then **Get started**.
2. Add a real photo, the dish name (for example "Paneer makhani with saffron rice"), a one-line description, and set the button to **Learn more** linking to `https://www.messify.me/meal-plans/`.
3. Click **Publish**. Google reviews each product before it shows, usually within a few days.

Add 4–6 dishes and swap them every month or two so the profile looks active.

## 10. Reviews: the most important part

Reviews are the biggest local ranking factor you control. Target **30 reviews in the first 90 days**, mostly from customers in Silicon Oasis and Academic City.

**Who to ask:** every subscriber after their 5th day, and every catering client the day after their event.

**WhatsApp message to send (copy and paste):**

```
Hi [Name], thanks for eating with Messify this week! If you're enjoying your meals, would you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps other students and professionals nearby find us: [your review link]
```

Get your review link from the profile: **Ask for reviews → Copy link**.

**Rules to stay safe:**
- Never offer discounts or free meals in exchange for reviews. Google removes them and can penalise the profile.
- Don't write reviews yourselves or ask staff and family to.
- Reply to **every** review within 48 hours, positive or negative.

**Reply templates:**
- Positive: "Thank you, [Name]! So glad the meals are making your week easier. See you tomorrow morning."
- Negative: "Sorry about this, [Name]. That's not the standard we aim for. Please message us on +971 50 251 3033 so we can make it right."
